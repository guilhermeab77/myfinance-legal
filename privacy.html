MyFinance Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. About MyFinance

MyFinance is a personal finance management application currently developed and used for private, non-commercial purposes. The application may be expanded to additional users in the future.

2. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: guilhermealexb@gmail.com .

3. Information Processed

MyFinance processes financial information entered or imported by the user, including account balances, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, categories, budgets, savings goals and financial forecasts.

4. Bank Account Connections

MyFinance is developing an integration with Revolut through an Open Banking provider, subject to the user's explicit authorisation.

The intended connection is read-only and is designed to retrieve account balances and transaction information. MyFinance does not intend to initiate payments or transfers.

5. Meal Card Integration

MyFinance also plans to support Edenred meal cards, including available balances, card top-ups and purchase transactions. Automatic connectivity has not yet been implemented or confirmed.

6. Data Storage

MyFinance uses Supabase for application authentication and data storage. Financial records are stored to support personal finance management and forecasting.

7. Data Use

Financial information is used to display account balances, categorise transactions, analyse spending, manage budgets and calculate financial forecasts.

8. Data Sharing

MyFinance does not sell personal financial information. Technical service providers may process data as necessary to operate the application and authorised integrations.

9. Security

MyFinance is designed to use authenticated access and database access controls. Banking credentials and private integration keys must not be exposed in the client-side application.

10. Data Access and Deletion

Requests concerning access, correction or deletion of personal information can be sent to guilhermealexb@gmail.com .

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as MyFinance develops new functionality or introduces additional integrations.
