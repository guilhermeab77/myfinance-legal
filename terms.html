MyFinance Terms of Use

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. About the Application

MyFinance is a personal finance management application currently developed for private, non-commercial use. It provides tools for tracking financial transactions, budgets, savings goals and financial forecasts.

2. Current Availability

MyFinance is currently under development and is not offered as a public commercial service. Features may change, be unavailable or contain errors.

3. Financial Information

MyFinance provides financial tracking, calculations and projections for informational purposes only. Forecasts are estimates and do not guarantee future account balances or financial outcomes.

4. Bank Connections

MyFinance plans to support connections to financial institutions through authorised third-party providers, subject to user consent and technical availability.

The intended bank connections are read-only. MyFinance does not currently provide payment initiation or money transfer services.

5. Meal Card Support

Support for meal cards, including Edenred, is planned. Availability of automatic data synchronisation depends on the relevant provider and integration.

6. Data Accuracy

Imported financial information may be delayed, incomplete or subject to correction. Users should verify important financial information directly with their financial institution.

7. Privacy

Information about the processing of personal data is available in the MyFinance Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to the Application

MyFinance may introduce new functionality, modify existing features or discontinue integrations as development progresses.
